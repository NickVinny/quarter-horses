Proposed direction · For discussion

A clear direction.
A few important choices.

This proposal explores a more focused, personal expression of Quarter Horses. It is a new direction for discussion, separate from the original sketches—not a final scope or a live vehicle offering.

Our recommendation

Begin with a polished landing page, a carefully selected gallery, and a direct path to a personal conversation. Add live inventory when it serves the business and someone can keep it accurate.

Confirm before launch

Confirm the business offering, public contact recipient, service area, and a short introduction to the person behind Quarter Horses. The current inquiry experience prepares a copyable draft; direct delivery is not connected. The proposal labels and this discussion guide belong to the review version and will be removed from the public visitor experience.

Photography sets the standard.

Use real vehicles with consistent lighting and accurate details. A small shoot at an authentic Kentucky location can establish the brand's roots more convincingly than a staged composite. Keep the landscape understated, and preserve the horse silhouette in the logo. The preview now uses the supplied customer photographs. The consistent setting, distinctive cars, and interior details give us a strong starting point. Please confirm permission to publish these images and provide original-resolution files where available.

01   What should the website do first?

Choose the strongest initial purpose. We can add capabilities later.

02   What additional photography is available?

We have received ten customer photos. Are original-resolution files, additional vehicle angles, showroom, team, or horse-country photographs available—and approved for publication?

03   Which cars can we feature?

For a sold-car gallery: choose representative cars and confirm they may be shown. For inventory: provide accurate descriptions, pricing preferences, availability, and matching photos.

04   Who will keep the content current?

Live inventory needs a named owner and timely updates when vehicles arrive, change price, or sell.

05   How should a visitor contact you?

Choose the channels you actively monitor. Confirm what services you actually offer, including sourcing, remote walkthroughs, or viewings.

06   What should this first release prioritize?

Consider brand presentation, qualified inquiries, displaying stock, or demonstrating a history of exceptional cars.

What happens next

Your answers establish the right scope. We then confirm photography, content, contact routing, ongoing ownership, and a tailored estimate before building the customer-ready release.
