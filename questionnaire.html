1. Design & Intent

This initial design uses real car photography, simple navigation, and consistent actions. Usability heuristics—clear choices, familiar controls, and minimal clutter—provide a foundation we can build on as the business needs become clearer.

01. What should the website accomplish?

Select the main purpose and describe the audience you want to reach.

02. Does the design fit Quarter Horses?

Comment on the logo, colors, typography, photo emphasis, spacing, and mobile experience.

03. What belongs in the gallery?

Confirm which vehicles can be shown and whether their status should be displayed.

04. What photography and brand material can you provide?

We have ten vehicle photos. Original files and consistent lighting will help maintain quality.

05. What should visitors do next?

Select the channels you actively monitor.

2. Solution Planning

Confirm the address, operating model, and requirements before choosing the final platform.

06. What website address and hosting arrangement do you prefer?

The domain is the public address; hosting is where the site runs. Confirm both, along with who owns the accounts.

07. Who will manage access, security, and maintenance?

Proposed baseline: HTTPS, multifactor authentication for administrators, limited access, backups, and a recovery owner. Confirm needs and ownership.

08. What data and integrations will the site use?

List only the data needed to handle inquiries and operate the site.

09. Which footer pages and controls apply?

For each item, identify approved content, an owner, or a decision still needed. Keep links only where they serve the business; privacy controls must connect to actual behavior.
